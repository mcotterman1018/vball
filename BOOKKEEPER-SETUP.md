# Bookkeeper setup — works without wifi

Send this to whoever is keeping the book. It takes about a minute, and it only
has to be done **once per device**.

> **Do this at home or anywhere with wifi/signal — not at the gym.** The one-time
> setup needs a connection. After that the app works with no signal at all.

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the link your coach sent you **in Safari** (it must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Wait for the "Keep a Scorebook" page to finish loading.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
5. You'll now have a **CourtIQ** icon on your home screen.

## Android — Chrome

1. Open the link your coach sent you in **Chrome**.
2. Wait for the "Keep a Scorebook" page to finish loading.
3. Tap the **⋮** menu in the top right.
4. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**), then confirm.
5. You'll now have a **CourtIQ** icon on your home screen.

## Samsung tablets & phones — Samsung Internet

Samsung devices open links in **Samsung Internet** by default. That works fine —
the steps are just in a different place.

1. Open the link your coach sent you in **Samsung Internet**.
2. Wait for the "Keep a Scorebook" page to finish loading.
3. Tap the **☰ menu** (three lines, usually bottom-right).
4. Tap **Add page to**, then **Home screen**, then confirm.
   - Some versions show a **⊕ / Install** icon right in the address bar instead —
     either one works.
5. You'll now have a **CourtIQ** icon on your home screen.

> If you'd rather use Chrome on a Samsung device, that works too — just follow
> the Chrome steps above. Only install it in **one** browser, though: a book kept
> in Chrome isn't visible to the Samsung Internet copy, and vice versa.

## Tablet tip

Turn the tablet **sideways (landscape)** while keeping the book. The scorebook
grid is wide, and landscape shows more of the rotation columns at once. It still
works in portrait — you'll just scroll sideways a bit more.

## On game day

- Open the app from the **CourtIQ icon**, not from a browser link.
- If there's no signal you'll see a yellow "Offline" note — that's fine, keep the
  book as normal. Everything saves on the tablet/phone as you go.
- Tap **Save match** at the end like usual. If you're offline it will say
  **"1 book waiting to upload"** — that's expected and the book is safe.
- Next time the device gets wifi, open the app and the book uploads on its own.
  The "waiting to upload" note disappears once it's done.

## Good to know

- **Don't delete the app** while it still says a book is waiting to upload — the
  book lives on the device until then.
- If the coach changes a roster after you've set up, you'll see the old roster
  until you open the app with a connection again.
- The plain link still works in any browser if you'd rather not install it. You
  just won't be able to open it with no signal.
